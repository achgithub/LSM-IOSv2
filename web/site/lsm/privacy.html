Privacy Policy

Last Stand Manager (Sports Manager) — last updated 19 August 2026

Last Stand Manager ("the app") is published by Sports Manager. It includes three game modes — Last Man Standing pools, Predictor prediction leagues and Killer (lives-based elimination) — all covered by this one policy. This policy explains what data the app collects and why.

No account required

You don't need to create an account or sign in to use the app. By default, your games, players, picks and predictions are stored locally on your device and are not sent to us. Two optional features send some data to our server: linking an email to your device (described below, for account recovery) and the player-link feature (described further down). Neither is required.

Email linking and device recovery (optional, free)

You can optionally link an email address to your device so you can recover your games if you lose or replace your phone. This isn't a login/password account — there's no session to sign in to. Linking works by one-time code: we email you a short code, and entering it in the app proves you own that address. We store your email address, linked to an internal account identifier and to the device currently allowed to act as that account. Only one device can be linked at a time — verifying a new device by email replaces the previous one.

Once linked, your games can sync to our server so a new device can pull them back down individually (you choose which game to restore, not a wholesale copy of everything). This is different from the player-link feature below: this syncs your own manager data, not players' submissions.

We send the one-time code via Resend, a transactional email provider — your email address is shared with Resend solely to deliver that code. See Resend's Privacy Policy. To unlink your email or delete the associated data, contact us at the address below.

League data

Fixtures, scores, standings and team data are fetched from our server, which in turn sources data from football-data.org. These requests don't include any personal information about you — they're anonymous reads of public sports data.

Player links and submissions (PWA, optional, paid tiers)

You can send individual players a personal link they can save to their phone's home screen to submit their own picks, predictions or (in Killer) build/hit choices each round, instead of you entering them by hand. When a player submits through that link, their name and pick/prediction/choice are sent to our server and held temporarily so you can review and approve them in the app; approved submissions are then copied into your local game data. We keep a rolling window of recent submissions on our server (older ones are pruned once a round is a few rounds old) and don't otherwise use this data. You can revoke a player's link at any time from within the app, which invalidates it.

Data availability between seasons

For each league, there is normally a short window of around one to two weeks between seasons (typically over the summer) while the new season's fixtures, teams and tables are being transferred onto our server. During this window the app may only be able to show the most recently cached data for that league rather than fresh updates. This doesn't affect games already in progress, and normal updates resume once the new season's data is in place.

Advertising

Free-tier use of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. Depending on your consent choice (shown via Google's User Messaging Platform on first launch in the UK/EEA, and via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt elsewhere), AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to show relevant ads, or non-personalised ads if you decline. You can change your tracking choice at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See Google's Privacy Policy for details on what AdMob collects.

Subscriptions

Paid tiers (No Ads / 3 Leagues / 5 Leagues / 7 Leagues) are sold as auto-renewing subscriptions through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Player links are bundled into the 3+ league tiers at no extra charge — there's no separate cloud purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple and managed on our behalf by RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous purchase/subscription identifier to validate your purchase and restore it across devices — we never see your card details. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

  • Deleting the app removes all locally-stored game data.
  • You can revoke a player's submission link at any time from within the app.
  • To unlink your email or delete the account data described above, email us at the address below.
  • You can opt out of personalised ads at any time via your device's tracking settings.
  • To cancel a subscription, use the App Store under your Apple ID → Subscriptions.
